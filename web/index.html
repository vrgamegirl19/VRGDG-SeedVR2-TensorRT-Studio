Render engines

SeedVR2 + TensorRT runs the SeedVR2 restoration pipeline with the TensorRT-accelerated VAE decoding path. It is the recommended fast option on supported NVIDIA systems.

SeedVR2 (Legacy) uses the standard SeedVR2 PyTorch path. It can support more temporal batch sizes, but it is substantially slower and may take a very long time on video. Use it at your own risk for long renders.

TensorRT currently supports temporal batches 5 and 21. The batch dropdown updates automatically when you switch engines.

Resumable long-video rendering

When enabled, the full video is divided into manageable chunks and processed one at a time. Completed chunks are saved immediately, with a small temporal overlap to reduce boundary seams.

If a render fails, the completed chunks remain on disk. The failure reason and backend log are saved, and Retry from last chunk continues from the first unfinished chunk automatically.

Auto (recommended) uses the video duration, frame rate/frame count, and temporal batch size to target roughly ten useful checkpoints, then selects the closest available chunk length. The chosen value is saved in the job manifest and render log.

This feature is off by default. It does not change the normal render quality or require any manual chunk assembly.
